Why a skin can look wrong

Minecraft textures use fixed UV regions. Drawing on the wrong rectangle can put an eye on the back of a head or move a shoe onto a leg. Nova's Focus and 3D editor exist to remove that guesswork.
128×128 skins use the same layout at higher pixel density. Larger custom textures are treated as advanced assets.
